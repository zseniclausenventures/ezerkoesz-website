Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 25, 2026  ·  Ezerkoesz Ventures AS

1. Who We Are

Ezerkoesz Ventures AS is a Norwegian digital product company registered in Bergen, Norway (Org.nr. 933 163 865). We operate the website ezerkoesz.com and sell digital products.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to process your purchase and deliver your product:

  • Name and email address (provided at checkout)
  • Payment information (processed by Lemon Squeezy — we never see your full card details)
  • Purchase history and product license records
  • Basic usage data collected via Cloudflare (IP address, browser type, page views)

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Process and fulfil your order
  • Send your product download link and order confirmation
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with Norwegian tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (a Stripe company), who acts as Merchant of Record. Please review their privacy policy at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy for details on how payment data is handled.

5. Data Retention

We retain purchase records for a minimum of 5 years in accordance with Norwegian accounting law (Bokføringsloven). You may request deletion of your personal data for non-accounting records at any time.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a resident of the EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@ezerkoesz.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

This website uses no tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set technical cookies necessary for security and performance. No third-party advertising or analytics cookies are used.
