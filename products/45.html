What Is This?

The AI ROI Command Center is a Notion template that transforms raw AI usage data into clear, actionable business intelligence. It gives you a single source of truth for every AI initiative across your organization so you can make confident decisions about what to scale, what to cut, and what to fix.

Who Is This For?

This template is built for three types of people who are tired of guessing whether their AI investments are paying off:

What's Included

### AI Initiative Tracker Main Database

The core of the template. Every AI tool, use case, and experiment lives here.

| Column | Type | Description |

|---|---|---|

| Initiative Name | Title | Name of the AI tool or use case (e.g. *GPT-4 Contract Summarization*) |

| Department | Select | Sales / Legal / HR / Finance / Ops / Engineering |

| Monthly Spend ($) | Number | Combined licensing + infrastructure cost per month |

| Hours Saved / Month | Number | Measured time reduction across the team |

| Error Rate (%) | Number | Percentage of outputs requiring human correction |

| ROI Score | Formula | Auto-calculated return on investment (see formula below) |

| Status | Select | At Risk / Review / Performing |

ROI Score Formula:

```

((Hours Saved Avg Hourly Rate) - Monthly Spend) / Monthly Spend 100

```

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Follow these steps to go from zero to a fully operational AI ROI dashboard:

Step 1 Duplicate the template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of the Notion page. The entire template will be copied to your workspace instantly.

Step 2 Add your first initiative

Open the AI Initiative Tracker database and click "+ New". Enter the name of one AI tool your team is currently using (e.g. *ChatGPT for Support Responses*).

Step 3 Fill in your numbers

Add your monthly spend, estimated hours saved per month, and current error rate. The ROI Score calculates automatically no formulas to set up.

Step 4 Set the status

Based on your ROI Score, assign a status: At Risk, Review, or Performing. Use this to prioritize your next action.

Step 5 Repeat for every active initiative

Add all your current AI tools and use cases. Once populated, your dashboard is live and ready to share with leadership.

Pro tip: Start with your top 3 most expensive AI tools. You'll often find an insight worth acting on within the first 10 minutes.

FAQ

Q: Do I need any technical skills to use this template?

No. Everything is built directly in Notion using native features databases, formulas, and views. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can use this template. No code, no integrations, no setup required beyond duplicating the page.

Q: How do I calculate "Hours Saved per Month" if I don't have exact data?

Start with a conservative estimate. Survey the team members using the tool and ask them how much time it saves them per week, then multiply by 4. Even rough estimates will give you a directionally useful ROI Score that you can refine over time.

Q: Can I add custom departments or status labels?

Yes. Every Select field in Notion is fully editable. Click any dropdown, select "Edit options", and add, rename, or recolor any label to match your organization's structure.

Q: Is this template suitable for small teams or startups, or only enterprises?

It works for any size. If you're using two or more AI tools and want to know which one is actually worth it, this template gives you that answer. Enterprises benefit from the multi-department overview; small teams benefit from the focus and simplicity.

Q: Can multiple people on my team work in the template at the same time?

Yes. Because this lives in Notion, you can share it with your entire team or just specific members. Assign initiative owners, leave comments, and collaborate in real time all within the same workspace.
