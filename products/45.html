What Is This?

The AI ROI Command Center is a Notion template that transforms raw AI usage data into clear, actionable investment decisions. It gives you a single source of truth for tracking costs, measuring ROI, and deciding which AI initiatives to scale, optimize, or shut down — without drowning in spreadsheets or vendor dashboards.

What's Included

### 📊 AI Initiative Tracker *(Primary Database)*

The core of the system. Every AI initiative lives here with full cost, ROI, and performance data attached.

| Field | Type | Description |

|---|---|---|

| Initiative Name | Title | e.g., "GPT-4 Customer Support Bot" |

| AI Tool / Model | Select | GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, Custom LLM, Copilot |

| Business Unit | Multi-select | Sales, Support, Marketing, Engineering, Finance, HR |

| Status | Select | 🔴 Underperforming / 🟡 Baseline / 🟢 Optimized / ⚫ Sunset |

| Monthly Cost (USD) | Number | Currency format |

| Measured ROI % | Number | Calculated from savings vs. cost |

| Time Saved (hrs/month) | Number | Human hours recovered per month |

### 📈 ROI Dashboard

Pre-built views that surface what matters instantly:

  • Portfolio Overview — All initiatives ranked by ROI %
  • Cost vs. Value Matrix — Identify overpaid underperformers at a glance
  • Status Board — Kanban view by initiative health (Underperforming → Optimized)
  • Business Unit Breakdown — Filtered views per department

### 🎯 Optimization Playbooks

Decision frameworks baked directly into the template:

  • When to Scale a model (criteria checklist)
  • When to Fix a model (root cause diagnostic)
  • When to Sunset a model (exit checklist)

### 📋 Monthly Review Log

A structured space to document quarterly AI reviews, capture decisions made, and track changes over time — so you always have an audit trail.

### 🧮 Cost Calculator Widget

Input your current AI tool costs and estimated time savings to automatically generate baseline ROI benchmarks before you even start tracking.

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Follow these steps to go from zero to your first ROI entry:

Step 1 — Duplicate the template

Click the "Duplicate" button at the top right of the Notion page. This copies the entire workspace into your own Notion account.

Step 2 — List your active AI tools

Open the AI Initiative Tracker database and add one row per AI tool or initiative currently in use. Don't worry about having all data — initiative name and business unit is enough to start.

Step 3 — Fill in cost data

Add your monthly spend per initiative. Check your credit card statements, vendor invoices, or IT procurement records. Even rough estimates are better than blanks.

Step 4 — Assign a Status

Give each initiative an honest status label: 🔴 Underperforming, 🟡 Baseline, 🟢 Optimized, or ⚫ Sunset. This single field unlocks the most useful dashboard views immediately.

Step 5 — Open the ROI Dashboard

Review the Cost vs. Value Matrix view. You now have a working overview of your AI portfolio. Schedule your first monthly review directly in the Monthly Review Log.

> ⏱️ *Most users complete their first full portfolio entry in under 15 minutes.*

FAQ

Q: Do I need any special Notion plan to use this template?

A: The template works on Notion's free plan. Some advanced features like database filtering across multiple workspaces may require a Plus or Business plan, but the core functionality is fully accessible for free.

---

Q: We use AI tools not listed in the dropdown options. Can I customize the template?

A: Absolutely. Every select and multi-select field is fully editable. Simply click any dropdown option to add, rename, or remove values to match your actual tech stack.

---

Q: We have 30+ AI initiatives across 8 departments. Will this still work at scale?

A: Yes. The template is designed with enterprise-scale in mind. Use the Business Unit filter to break your portfolio into department-specific views, and use the Status board to prioritize where attention is needed most. You can also duplicate views and apply custom filters per team.

---

Q: How do I calculate ROI % if I don't have precise savings data?

A: Start with time saved. Multiply hours saved per month by the average hourly cost of the employee whose time was recovered. Compare that number against your monthly tool cost. Even a conservative estimate gives you a directional ROI figure that is far more useful than no data at all. The template includes a built-in Cost Calculator to guide you through this.

---

Q: Can I share this with my team or use it across multiple workspaces?

A: Once duplicated, the template is yours to share, modify, and distribute within your organization. You can add teammates directly to the Notion page and assign editing or viewing permissions as needed.
