📌 What Is This?

The SaaS Pricing Revenue Maximizer is a complete Notion workspace that helps SaaS founders and product teams benchmark competitors, run 6 battle-tested pricing models, and identify the revenue gaps silently killing their growth. It turns complex pricing strategy into a structured, repeatable process you can execute in a single afternoon.

### 💰 The 6 Pricing Model Frameworks

Each model comes with a dedicated workspace section including a description, pros/cons matrix, ideal use case, and a DIY calculation template:

1. Flat-Rate Pricing — One price, one product, maximum simplicity

2. Per-Seat / Per-User Pricing — Scale revenue directly with your customer's team growth

3. Usage-Based Pricing — Align cost with value delivered; charge for what customers actually consume

4. Freemium Model — Acquire users at zero cost, convert them at scale

5. Tiered Pricing — Segment your market with Good / Better / Best plan architecture

6. Hybrid Pricing — Combine models for maximum revenue capture across customer segments

⚡ Get Started in 3 Minutes

Follow these four steps and you'll have a working pricing strategy session running before your next coffee break:

Step 1 — Duplicate the Template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of the Notion page to save a full copy to your own Notion workspace.

Step 2 — Add Your Top 5 Competitors

Open the Competitor Price Tracker database and add your 5 closest competitors. Fill in the pricing columns using their public pricing pages. Spend no more than 10 minutes here — rough numbers are fine to start.

Step 3 — Select Your Candidate Pricing Models

Navigate to the Pricing Model Comparison Matrix and check off the 2–3 models that most align with how your product delivers value. Each model section contains a guided template with prompts to help you think through the fit.

Step 4 — Run the Revenue Gap Analyzer

Input your current pricing into the Revenue Gap Analyzer and compare it against the benchmarks you collected in Step 2. The section will surface where you're positioned versus the market — and where the opportunity is.

> 💡 Pro Tip: Don't try to do everything at once. Start with the Competitor Tracker and one pricing model. Clarity compounds quickly once you see real numbers on the page.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need any paid Notion plan to use this template?

No. This template works on Notion's free plan. All databases, views, and pages are compatible with free accounts. Some advanced Notion features like unlimited version history require a paid plan, but nothing in the core template requires an upgrade.

2. I'm pre-launch and don't have any pricing yet. Is this still useful?

Absolutely — in fact, this is the ideal time to use it. The Competitor Price Tracker and Pricing Model Frameworks are specifically designed to help you establish a defensible pricing strategy *before* launch, so you're not stuck reversing a bad pricing decision after you've acquired your first customers.

3. Can I use this for a B2C product or does it only apply to B2B SaaS?

The template is optimized for B2B SaaS, but the core frameworks (especially Freemium, Usage-Based, and Flat-Rate) are directly applicable to B2C subscription products. The competitor benchmarking structure works for any software product with publicly available pricing.

4. How often should I update the Competitor Price Tracker?

A good rule of thumb is once per quarter for regular maintenance, and immediately whenever a direct competitor makes a public pricing change. Use the Pricing Change Log section to record both competitor updates and your own responses — over time this becomes a valuable strategic record.

5. Can I share or resell this template?

This template is licensed for personal and internal commercial use. You may use it freely within your own company or for your own products.
