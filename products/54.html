What Is This?

LinkVault Pro is a ready-to-use Obsidian vault designed to replace your chaotic browser bookmarks with a structured, searchable knowledge system. Instead of dumping links into endless bookmark folders you never revisit, LinkVault Pro gives you a consistent workflow for capturing, categorizing, tagging, and surfacing your best resources — exactly when you need them.

No more "I saved that article somewhere..." moments.

---

Who Is This For?

LinkVault Pro is built for:

  • 🔬 Researchers who collect papers, articles, and references across multiple projects
  • 🧑‍💻 Developers & designers who constantly save tools, tutorials, and documentation
  • 📈 Business professionals who curate industry news, reports, and strategy resources
  • 🧠 Knowledge workers who want a second brain — not just a bookmark graveyard
  • 📚 Lifelong learners who read widely but struggle to retrieve what they've saved

If you've ever lost a link you *know* you saved, this vault is for you.

5-Minute Setup

> Prerequisites: [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) installed on your device.

### Step 1 — Download & Open (1 min)

1. Download or clone this repository

2. Open Obsidian → Open folder as vault

3. Select the `LinkVault Pro` folder

### Step 2 — Install Required Plugins (2 min)

1. Go to Settings → Community Plugins → Browse

2. Search for and install each plugin listed in the [Required Plugins](#required-plugins) section below

3. Enable all installed plugins

### Step 3 — Configure Your Quick Capture Shortcut (1 min)

1. Open Settings → Hotkeys

2. Search for `Templates: Insert template`

3. Assign your preferred shortcut (recommended: `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + L`)

4. Point it to `_templates/link-note-template.md`

> 💡 Pain point solved: This replaces the need to manually decide where a link goes every time you save it. Paste your URL, hit your shortcut, fill in two fields, and you're done. Categorization and tagging happens on your own schedule during the weekly review.

### Step 4 — Save Your First Link (1 min)

1. Navigate to `📥 00-INBOX/_daily-capture.md`

2. Paste a URL

3. Use your new hotkey to insert the link note template

4. Fill in the title and optionally a one-line summary

5. That's it — process it later

Required Plugins

All plugins are free and available in the Obsidian Community Plugin browser.

| Plugin | Purpose | Required |

|---|---|---|

| [Dataview](https://github.com/blacksmithgu/obsidian-dataview) | Powers automatic link lists and dashboards | ✅ Yes |

| [Templater](https://github.com/SilentVoid13/Templater) | Smart templates with auto-fill for dates and metadata | ✅ Yes |

| [Tag Wrangler](https://github.com/pjeby/tag-wrangler) | Rename, merge, and manage tags across the vault | ✅ Yes |

| [QuickAdd](https://github.com/chhoumann/quickadd) | One-command link capture from anywhere in Obsidian | ⭐ Recommended |

| [Kanban](https://github.com/mgmeyers/obsidian-kanban) | Visual board for managing links in review | ⭐ Recommended |

| [Style Settings](https://github.com/mgmeyers/obsidian-style-settings) | Customize vault appearance | Optional |
