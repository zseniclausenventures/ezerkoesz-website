What Is This?

PayFlow AI is a plug-and-play spreadsheet-based command center built for finance teams, operations managers, and compliance officers who are tired of chasing approvals through email chains, Slack threads, and sticky notes.

Most AI workflow templates completely ignore the payment approval and compliance space. PayFlow AI fills that gap — giving you a structured, automated, audit-ready system that works out of the box.

No coding. No expensive software. No onboarding calls.

---

What Problem Does This Solve?

If any of these sound familiar, this was built for you:

  • ❌ Payment requests sit in someone's inbox for days before anyone acts on them
  • ❌ You have no single place to see what's approved, pending, or rejected
  • ❌ Compliance documentation is scattered across folders, emails, and shared drives
  • ❌ Audit time turns into a full-week scramble to find records
  • ❌ Approvers forget to sign off because there's no structured escalation process
  • ❌ You've looked for AI workflow templates for payment approval — and found nothing useful

PayFlow AI replaces that chaos with a single source of truth.

What's Included

### Sheets & Tabs

| Tab | What It Does |

|-----|-------------|

| 🏠 START HERE | Setup wizard and quick-start guide — get running in under 3 minutes |

| 📥 Payment Requests | Central intake log for all incoming payment requests |

| ✅ Approval Tracker | Live status view with bottleneck identification per request |

| 🔍 Compliance Log | Audit-ready record of every action taken on every request |

| 📊 Dashboard | Visual overview of approval rates, cycle times, and pending volume |

| ⚙️ Settings | Configure approvers, thresholds, departments, and escalation rules |

### Built-In Features

  • Multi-tier approval logic — set up to 3 approval levels based on payment amount
  • Status tracking — Pending / Under Review / Approved / Rejected / Escalated
  • Automatic escalation flags — highlights requests that have been waiting too long
  • Compliance-ready audit trail — every status change is timestamped and logged
  • Conditional formatting — color-coded priority and risk levels at a glance
  • Dropdown menus and data validation — reduces input errors from the start
  • Department and vendor filtering — find any request in seconds
  • Pre-built AI prompt templates — use with ChatGPT or Copilot to draft approval summaries, flag anomalies, and generate compliance reports
