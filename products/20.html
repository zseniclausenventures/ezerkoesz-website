Most TCO calculators cover three or four cost lines. This one covers everything:

3. Customization and development both pre- and post-go-live

9. Upgrade and version costs mandatory upgrades are not free

11. Downtime and productivity loss modeled against your revenue per day

12. Contingency buffer because something always goes over budget
