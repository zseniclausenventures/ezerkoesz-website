What Problem Does This Solve?

ERP buyers consistently underestimate total cost of ownership because vendors quote only what's convenient. The real cost picture is scattered across:

  • IT infrastructure and hosting
  • Annual maintenance and support fees (typically 18–22% of license cost)
  • Implementation partner fees
  • Internal staff time during rollout
  • Customization and integration work
  • User training — initial and ongoing
  • Version upgrades (perpetual) or forced feature updates (SaaS)
  • Hidden ramp-up costs in year 1

Without a side-by-side framework, comparing a perpetual (on-premise) model to a SaaS subscription is like comparing a mortgage to rent — they look nothing alike on paper, but one is clearly better for *your* situation.

This calculator does that comparison. Completely. Transparently.
