What Is This?

The SaaS Pain Point Finder is a Notion template designed to systematically capture, organize, and analyze customer discovery interviews. It transforms raw conversations into actionable product intelligence — so your roadmap is driven by real pain points, not assumptions.

---

Who Is This For?

This template is built for:

  • 🧑‍💻 SaaS Founders & Product Managers who run customer interviews and need a structured system to track insights, patterns, and decisions across multiple conversations.
  • 📣 Growth & Marketing Teams who want to understand customer language, objections, and motivations to sharpen positioning and messaging.
  • 🔬 UX Researchers & Customer Success Managers who conduct regular discovery sessions and need a single source of truth for qualitative feedback.

What's Included

### 🗂️ Customer Discovery Tracker *(Main Database)*

The central hub for all your interviews. Every contact gets a dedicated record with:

| Field | Description |

|---|---|

| Contact Name | Full name of the interviewee |

| Company & Role | e.g. *Acme Corp — Head of Marketing* |

| Customer Segment | SMB / Mid-Market / Enterprise / Startup |

| Interview Status | 🔴 Not Started · 🟡 Scheduled · 🟢 Completed · ⚫ No-show |

| Top Pain Point | Multi-select tags for fast pattern recognition |

| Key Quote | The single most valuable thing they said |

| Follow-up Required | Checkbox + next action |

### 📋 Interview Question Bank

A ready-to-use library of proven discovery questions organized by category:

  • Problem exploration
  • Current workflow & workarounds
  • Budget & decision-making
  • Switching triggers

### 🔥 Pain Point Aggregator

A filtered view that groups recurring themes across all interviews — instantly revealing which problems appear most frequently and deserve your attention first.

### 🗺️ Insight-to-Roadmap Bridge

Link validated pain points directly to feature ideas. Prioritize ruthlessly based on frequency, severity, and segment.

### 📊 Interview Dashboard

A high-level overview showing:

  • Total interviews completed
  • Segment breakdown
  • Top 5 pain points by frequency
  • Interview pipeline status

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Step 1 — Duplicate the template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of this Notion page to add it to your workspace.

Step 2 — Add your first contact

Open the *Customer Discovery Tracker* database and create a new entry. Fill in the contact's name, company, role, and segment. Set the status to 🟡 Scheduled.

Step 3 — Prepare your interview

Open the *Interview Question Bank* and copy the relevant questions into a new page linked to your contact record. Customize freely based on your product and segment.

Step 4 — Run the interview & capture insights

During or immediately after the call, fill in the Top Pain Point, Key Quote, and any follow-up actions. Mark the status as 🟢 Completed.

Step 5 — Spot the patterns

After 5+ interviews, open the *Pain Point Aggregator* view to see which problems keep surfacing. Use the *Insight-to-Roadmap Bridge* to connect validated pain points to your next sprint.

> 💡 Pro tip: Aim for 10–15 interviews per customer segment before drawing conclusions. Patterns become clear fast.

FAQ

Q: Do I need any Notion experience to use this template?

A: No. The template is fully set up and ready to use out of the box. If you know how to create a new row in a table and fill in text fields, you have everything you need.

---

Q: Can I customize the pain point tags and customer segments?

A: Absolutely. All select and multi-select fields are fully editable. Add, rename, or remove any option to match your specific product category and market.

---

Q: How many interviews should I run before it becomes useful?

A: You'll start seeing value after your very first interview. Meaningful patterns typically emerge after 8–12 conversations. The Pain Point Aggregator becomes especially powerful at that point.

---

Q: Can I share this with my team?

A: Yes. Once duplicated to your workspace, you can share the page with teammates and collaborate in real time — just like any other Notion page.

---

Q: Is this template suitable for B2B SaaS only, or does it work for B2C as well?

A: It was designed with B2B SaaS in mind (segments like SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise reflect that), but it adapts easily to any product discovery workflow. Simply update the segment labels and question bank to fit your context.
