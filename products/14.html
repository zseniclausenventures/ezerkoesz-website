Get Started in 3 Minutes

No onboarding calls. No tutorials. Follow these steps and you'll be running your first interview today.

Step 1 — Duplicate the Template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of this Notion page. Select the workspace where you want to save it. Done.

Step 2 — Define Your ICP Segment

Open the *Customer Discovery Tracker* and decide which segment you're targeting first. Use the existing options (Early-Stage Founder / Growth PM / Enterprise Buyer / Solo Operator) or rename them to match your market.

Step 3 — Add Your First 5 Contacts

Add the names and companies of 5 people you want to interview. Set their status to *Not Scheduled* and assign them to an ICP segment. This becomes your outreach hit list.

Step 4 — Open the Interview Script

Navigate to the *Interview Script Templates* section. Pick the script that matches your segment. Read it once before your call — you don't need to memorize it.

Step 5 — Run the Interview & Log the Pain

After each call, open the contact's record and fill in the *Top Pain Point* field. Then add the full details to the *Pain Point Extraction Log*. That's it — your insights are now searchable and organized.

Step 6 — Find the Patterns

After 5–10 interviews, open the *Pattern Recognition Dashboard*. Look for pain points that appear 3+ times. Those are your building blocks.

FAQ

Q: Do I need to know how to use Notion to get value from this?

A: No. If you can type and click, you can use this template. Everything is pre-built — you just fill in your contacts and interview notes. Most users are fully operational within 10 minutes of duplicating it.

---

Q: Can I customize the ICP segments to fit my business?

A: Absolutely. The segment names are fully editable Select fields inside Notion. Rename, add, or remove segments to match your exact market. The template adapts to your business — not the other way around.

---

Q: How many interviews do I need to run before I see useful patterns?

A: Most founders start seeing clear signal after 5–7 interviews within the same ICP segment. The Pain Point Extraction Log is designed to make patterns visible quickly — you don't need 50 conversations to find your core insights.

---

Q: Is this only for pre-product startups, or can established SaaS teams use it?

A: Both. Pre-product founders use it to validate ideas before building. Established teams use it to challenge roadmap assumptions, explore new segments, or prepare for a major pivot. The system scales with your stage.

---

Q: What if I already have customer notes in another tool — can I migrate them?

A: Yes. The database structure is simple enough that you can copy-paste existing notes directly into the *Top Pain Point* and *Pain Point Extraction Log* fields. There's no proprietary format — plain text works perfectly.
