What Is This?

The SaaS Pain Point Finder is a structured Notion template that centralizes your entire customer discovery process in one place. It turns raw interviews and scattered notes into clear, actionable insight about who your best customers are and exactly what keeps them up at night.

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Follow these steps immediately after duplicating the template:

Step 1 — Duplicate the template

Click "Duplicate" in the top-right corner of this Notion page. It will be saved directly to your workspace.

Step 2 — Define your segments

Open the Customer Discovery Hub database and customize the `Customer Segment` property to match your business (e.g. Trial User, Enterprise, SMB, Churned).

Step 3 — Add your first contact

Create a new entry in the Customer Discovery Hub. Fill in the contact name, company, role, and set the interview status to `Scheduled`.

Step 4 — Run your interview

Open the Interview Templates section and pick the framework that fits your goal — discovery, churn, or win/loss. Copy the questions into your call notes.

Step 5 — Log the pain point

After the call, return to the contact entry. Select the `Top Pain Point` category, rate the `Pain Intensity`, and paste your key quotes into the notes field.

Step 6 — Review your dashboard

After 5+ interviews, open the Insight Dashboard to see patterns forming. Use the ICP Builder to sharpen your customer profile based on real data.

> 💡 *Most users complete their first full interview cycle within the first week. Aim for 10 interviews before drawing conclusions.*

FAQ

Q: Do I need any prior experience with Notion to use this?

A: No. The template is fully pre-built and ready to use. As long as you know how to duplicate a Notion page and type in a database, you're good to go. No setup, no configuration required.

---

Q: How many interviews should I run before the data becomes useful?

A: You'll start seeing themes after 5–7 interviews. Ten is the sweet spot for early-stage validation. For established products doing churn analysis or ICP refinement, aim for 15–20 before drawing strong conclusions.

---

Q: Can I share this with my team?

A: Absolutely. Since this lives in Notion, you can invite teammates to your workspace or share the page with editing access. Multiple people can log interviews, add contacts, and update statuses simultaneously.

---

Q: Can I customize the pain point categories and customer segments?

A: Yes — and you should. The default categories (Workflow, Pricing, Onboarding, Integration, Reporting) are starting points. Open any `Select` property and add, rename, or remove options to match your product and market.

---

Q: Is this a one-time purchase or a subscription?

A: This is a one-time purchase. You duplicate it once and own it forever. There are no recurring fees, no logins, and no platform lock-in beyond Notion itself (which has a generous free plan).
