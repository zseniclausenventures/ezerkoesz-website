What Is This?

The SaaS Pain Point Profiler is a structured Notion workspace that helps you run disciplined customer discovery interviews and turn raw conversations into actionable product and sales insights. It replaces scattered notes, forgotten follow-ups, and gut-feeling assumptions with a single source of truth for everything you learn about your customers.

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Step 1 — Duplicate the template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of this Notion page. The entire workspace will be copied to your account instantly.

Step 2 — Add your first contact

Open the Customer Discovery Hub database and create a new entry. Fill in the Contact Name, Company, and ICP Segment. Don't overthink it — you can always update fields as you learn more.

Step 3 — Pick your interview status

Set the Interview Status field to `Scheduled` or `Not Started`. If you already have someone in mind, book the call and come back to log your notes immediately after.

Step 4 — Run the interview

Navigate to the Interview Script & Question Bank section and copy the questions that fit your stage. Use the open-ended questions to let the customer talk — your job is to listen and capture their exact words.

Step 5 — Score the pain and log insights

After each interview, return to the contact's entry and fill in the Pain Score (1–10), the Primary Pain Category, and any raw quotes or observations. Add recurring themes to the Pain Point Library as they emerge.

FAQ

Q: Do I need to be technical to use this template?

Not at all. This template is built entirely in Notion using databases, select fields, and simple views. If you can fill out a form and take notes, you can use this system effectively from day one.

---

Q: How many interviews should I run before the data becomes useful?

Most founders start seeing clear patterns after 5–8 interviews within the same ICP segment. The Pain Score Dashboard will help you spot when a specific pain category is showing up consistently — that's your signal to dig deeper or start validating solutions.

---

Q: Can I use this for B2C products, not just B2B SaaS?

The template is optimized for B2B SaaS discovery, where understanding company context, role-based pain, and buying dynamics matters most. You can adapt it for B2C, but some fields (like ICP Segment and Company) may be less relevant depending on your use case.

---

Q: What if I'm doing discovery with my existing customers, not just prospects?

This template works equally well for existing customers. In fact, interviewing paying customers — especially churned ones — often produces the highest-quality pain data. Simply adjust the ICP Segment and Interview Status fields to reflect their current relationship with your product.

---

Q: Can I share this workspace with my team?

Yes. Since this lives in Notion, you can share the duplicated workspace with teammates directly through Notion's standard sharing settings. Multiple people can log interviews simultaneously, making it ideal for founding teams or small research squads running parallel discovery sprints.
