What's Included

The spreadsheet is organized into 6 purpose-built tabs, each color-coded for fast navigation:

### 🚀 `START HERE` — Green

Your onboarding hub. Step-by-step instructions, a quick-start checklist, and a map of the entire model. Start here every time.

### ⚙️ `ASSUMPTIONS` — Blue

The single source of truth for every input in the model. Change a number here and it flows through the entire spreadsheet automatically. No manual updates. No broken references.

Includes:

  • Monthly pricing per plan
  • Expected growth rates
  • Churn and expansion MRR assumptions
  • COGS and operating cost drivers
  • Headcount plan inputs

### 📊 `P&L MODEL` — White

A full 24-month monthly income statement built for SaaS. Tracks MRR, ARR, gross margin, operating expenses, and EBITDA — formatted the way investors expect to see it.

### 💰 `UNIT ECONOMICS` — Orange

The metrics that actually tell you if your business works:

  • LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)
  • CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
  • LTV:CAC Ratio
  • Payback Period
  • Magic Number

All calculated automatically from your assumptions.

### 📈 `PRICING SCENARIOS` — Purple

Model the revenue impact of 3 different pricing tiers side by side. Instantly see how price changes affect MRR, ARR, and margin — before you commit.

### 🔄 `COHORT ANALYSIS` — Yellow

Track monthly retention across customer cohorts. Visualize where churn happens, when customers expand, and how retention trends over time. Export-ready for investor decks.
