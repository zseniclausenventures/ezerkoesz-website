Finance clarity. Master your unit economics effortlessly.

What Is This?

The SaaS Profit Clarity Dashboard is a Notion template that gives SaaS founders and operators a complete unit economics engine — built directly inside their existing workspace. It automatically calculates your most critical profitability metrics (LTV, CAC, payback period, churn, and growth runway) so you can make confident, data-driven decisions without touching a spreadsheet.

---

Who Is This For?

This template was built for three types of people:

🚀 Early-Stage SaaS Founders

You're pre-Series A and need to understand whether your acquisition spend is sustainable. You know unit economics matter but don't have a finance team to build the models for you.

📈 Growth Marketers & Demand Gen Leads

You're running paid and organic campaigns across multiple channels and need a clear, side-by-side view of which cohorts are actually profitable — not just which ones convert.

💼 Bootstrapped Operators & Indie Hackers

You're watching every dollar. This template tells you exactly how long it takes to recoup your customer acquisition cost and how much runway each cohort generates.

What's Included

### 📊 Core Database — Cohort Economics Tracker

Track every customer cohort from day one. Each cohort entry captures:

  • Cohort Name — Descriptive label (e.g., *Q1 2024 — Paid LinkedIn*)
  • Acquisition Channel — Paid, Organic, Referral, Outbound, or Partner
  • Customers Acquired — Total customers in the cohort
  • Total CAC Spend ($) — All costs associated with acquiring the cohort
  • CAC Per Customer ($) *(auto-calculated)* — Total spend divided by customers acquired
  • Monthly Recurring Revenue ($) — Average MRR per customer in the cohort
  • Gross Margin (%) — Your product margin, used in LTV calculations
  • Average Customer Lifespan (months) — Expected or measured retention duration
  • LTV ($) *(auto-calculated)* — Lifetime value based on MRR, margin, and lifespan
  • LTV:CAC Ratio *(auto-calculated)* — The single most important SaaS health metric
  • CAC Payback Period (months) *(auto-calculated)* — How long until each customer pays back their acquisition cost
  • Churn Rate (%) — Monthly churn for the cohort
  • Growth Runway Score — A composite indicator of cohort sustainability

### 📋 Dashboard Views Included

  • All Cohorts Overview — Full table with all metrics visible at a glance
  • By Acquisition Channel — Grouped view to compare Paid vs. Organic vs. Referral performance
  • LTV:CAC Health Filter — Automatically surfaces cohorts below the 3:1 danger threshold
  • Payback Period Ranking — Sorted view to identify your fastest and slowest-recovering cohorts
  • Churn Risk Monitor — Flags cohorts with churn rates above your defined threshold

### 🧮 Built-In Formula Engine

All key metrics are calculated automatically using Notion formulas. No manual updates, no copy-pasting from spreadsheets. You enter the raw numbers — the dashboard does the rest.

### 📖 Setup Guide & Benchmark Reference

Includes an embedded reference page with:

  • Industry benchmarks for LTV:CAC, payback period, and churn by SaaS stage
  • Formula logic explained in plain English
  • Tips for inputting historical cohort data

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Step 1 — Duplicate the Template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of this Notion page. The entire template — all databases, views, and formulas — will be copied directly into your workspace.

Step 2 — Enter Your First Cohort

Open the `📊 Cohort Economics Tracker` database and click "+ New". Name your first cohort (e.g., *Q1 2024 — Google Ads*), select your acquisition channel, and fill in your raw numbers: customers acquired, total CAC spend, MRR per customer, gross margin, and churn rate.

Step 3 — Let the Formulas Work

Once your raw inputs are saved, every calculated field — CAC per customer, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio, and payback period — will populate automatically. No extra steps required.

> ✅ You're done. Navigate to the Dashboard view to see your full unit economics picture. Add more cohorts as your data grows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need any technical skills or Notion experience to use this?

No. If you know how to type into a table and select from a dropdown, you can use this template. All formulas are pre-built and locked — you only interact with the input fields. A brief setup guide is included to walk you through every field.

---

Q: Can I track multiple acquisition channels and compare them?

Yes, and this is one of the template's core strengths. Every cohort is tagged with an acquisition channel (Paid, Organic, Referral, Outbound, or Partner), and there is a dedicated By Channel grouped view that lets you compare LTV:CAC ratios and payback periods side-by-side across channels.

---

Q: What if I don't have all my historical data ready?

You can start with whatever data you have. The template is designed to work with partial inputs — enter what you know, and calculated fields will update as you add more. You can also use estimated or projected numbers and refine them as actuals become available.

---

Q: Will this work if I'm on Notion's free plan?

Yes. This template uses only core Notion features — databases, formulas, and filtered views — that are available on all plans, including the free tier. No paid integrations or third-party tools are required.

---

Q: Can I customize the template to fit my business model?

Absolutely. The template is fully editable. You can rename columns, add custom properties, create new filtered views, or adjust formula thresholds to match your specific margins and business model. The core formula logic is documented in the included setup guide so you understand exactly what to change and why.
