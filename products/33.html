What Is This?

The Profit First Pricing Engine is a done-for-you Notion template that automatically calculates tiered service packages so you always know your true profitable price before quoting a client. It takes the guesswork out of pricing by combining your time, costs, and profit targets into one clean, automated system.

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Step 1 — Duplicate the template

Click the Duplicate button in the top-right corner of the Notion page to add it to your workspace.

Step 2 — Set your default hourly rate

Open the Pricing Dashboard and enter your target hourly rate. This becomes the baseline for all formula calculations across the template.

Step 3 — Add your first service

Go to the Service Packages Master database, click New, and fill in the Package Name, Service Category, Tier, and estimated hours. Every other field calculates automatically.

FAQ

1. Do I need any Notion experience to use this?

No. The template is fully set up and ready to use out of the box. If you know how to type into a table and select from a dropdown, you have everything you need. All formulas are already built in — you never have to touch them.

2. Can I add my own service categories?

Absolutely. The Service Category and Tier fields are standard Notion Select properties, meaning you can add, rename, or remove any option to match your business. Nothing is locked.

3. What if my pricing includes software, subcontractors, or other hard costs?

The Raw Cost formula is designed to be extended. You can add additional Number columns for things like software licences, contractor fees, or material costs, and reference them in the formula. A short guide for this is included inside the template itself.

4. Will this work for retainers and monthly packages, not just one-off projects?

Yes. Simply enter the monthly estimated hours in the Your Time (hrs) field and treat the output as your monthly retainer price. Many users duplicate the template section and label it specifically for retainers to keep things organised.

5. Can I use this for multiple businesses or client accounts?

Each duplicated copy of the template operates independently in your Notion workspace. If you manage pricing for multiple brands or clients, simply duplicate the template once per business and rename each copy accordingly.
