What Is This?

The Profit First Pricing Engine is a Notion template that automatically calculates tiered service packages using Profit First percentages — so you always know your true minimum viable price before you send a single proposal. It eliminates the guesswork from pricing by building your profit, taxes, and operating expenses directly into every number you charge.

What's Included

### 📊 Main Database — Service Package Calculator

The core of the template. Each row represents a service or package with the following fields:

| Field | Type | Description |

|---|---|---|

| Service Name | Title | Name of your service or package tier |

| Delivery Hours | Number | Estimated hours to complete the work |

| Hourly Rate | Number | Your base hourly rate (USD) |

| Raw Revenue Target | Formula | Auto-calculates: `Delivery Hours × Hourly Rate` |

| Profit % Allocation | Number | Your target Profit First profit percentage |

| Tax % Allocation | Number | Percentage set aside for taxes |

| Operating % Allocation | Number | Percentage for overhead and expenses |

| Owner Pay % Allocation | Number | Your actual take-home pay percentage |

| Minimum Viable Price | Formula | The lowest price you can charge and still hit all allocations |

| Recommended Price | Formula | Suggested price with a healthy buffer built in |

| Package Tier | Select | Starter / Growth / Premium classification |

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Step 1 — Duplicate the Template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of this Notion page. It will be added directly to your Notion workspace.

Step 2 — Set Your Hourly Rate

Open the `Service Package Calculator` database and update the Hourly Rate field to reflect what you currently charge (or what you *want* to charge).

Step 3 — Enter Your Profit First Percentages

Fill in your Profit %, Tax %, Operating %, and Owner Pay % columns. If you're new to Profit First, start with: Profit 5% / Tax 15% / Operating 30% / Owner Pay 50%.

Step 4 — Add Your Services

Create a new row for each service or package you offer. Enter the estimated delivery hours and let the formulas do the rest.

Step 5 — Review Your Minimum Viable Prices

Check the Minimum Viable Price and Recommended Price columns. If your current prices are below these numbers — you now know exactly what to fix.

> ✅ That's it. You now have a pricing system that works *for* you, not against you.

FAQ

❓ Do I need to know Profit First to use this template?

No prior knowledge required. The template is built around Profit First principles, but everything is explained inside the Setup Guide page. You just need to know what percentage of your revenue you want to keep as profit — even starting at 1% is a win.

❓ Can I use this for non-USD currencies?

Yes. The formulas are currency-agnostic — they work with numbers, not currency symbols. Simply input your prices in whatever currency you use and the calculations will be accurate.

❓ What if I charge project-based rates instead of hourly?

The hourly rate field is used as a *baseline input* to calculate your minimum viable price. If you charge flat project fees, you can enter your estimated hourly equivalent (total project budget ÷ expected hours) to reverse-engineer whether your flat fee is profitable.

❓ Can I add more package tiers beyond Starter / Growth / Premium?

Absolutely. The Package Tier field is a Select property — you can add, rename, or remove any tier directly in Notion. The comparison view will update automatically.

❓ Will this work on the free Notion plan?

Yes. This template uses standard Notion databases, formulas, and views — no advanced features that require a paid plan. It works on all Notion tiers, including the free plan.
