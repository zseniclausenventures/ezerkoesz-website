What Is This?

Handmade Profit Engine is a Notion-based pricing and profit calculator built specifically for handmade sellers. It automatically calculates your net profit after materials, labor, packaging, shipping, and platform fees — giving you complete financial clarity before you publish a single listing.

What's Included

### 📊 Product Pricing Calculator (Main Database)

The core of the template. Every row is a product. Every column does the math for you.

| Column | Type | What It Tracks |

|---|---|---|

| Product Name | Title | The name of your item |

| Material Cost | Number (USD) | Raw materials per unit |

| Labor Cost | Number (USD) | Your hourly rate × time spent |

| Packaging Cost | Number (USD) | Boxes, tissue paper, stickers, tape |

| Shipping Cost | Number (USD) | Actual postage per order |

| Platform Fee % | Number (%) | Etsy 6.5%, Shopify 2%, etc. |

| Sale Price | Number (USD) | Your listed selling price |

| Net Profit | Formula | Auto-calculated after all deductions |

| Profit Margin % | Formula | Instantly see if your pricing is healthy |

### 📅 Monthly Revenue Tracker

Log your sales by month and watch your income trends emerge. Know your best-selling months, plan for slow seasons, and set realistic revenue goals.

### 🏷️ Platform Fee Reference Sheet

A pre-filled cheat sheet with common platform fees (Etsy, Shopify, Amazon Handmade, Not on the High Street, and more) — no more Googling every time you list somewhere new.

### 💡 Hourly Rate Calculator

Not sure what to charge for your time? This section helps you determine a fair labor rate based on your income goals and available working hours.

### 🎯 Pricing Strategy Guide

A built-in reference page explaining the difference between cost-plus pricing, value-based pricing, and competitive pricing — with examples tailored to handmade sellers.

Get Started in 3 Minutes

Step 1 — Duplicate the Template

Click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner of the Notion template page. It will be added directly to your Notion workspace.

Step 2 — Set Your Labor Rate

Navigate to the Hourly Rate Calculator section. Enter your target monthly income and weekly working hours. Your suggested hourly rate is calculated automatically.

Step 3 — Add Your First Product

Open the Product Pricing Calculator database. Click "+ New" and fill in your material cost, labor time, packaging, shipping, and your sale price. The net profit and margin calculate instantly.

> ✅ That's it. You now know exactly what you earn from that product.

FAQ

❓ Do I need a paid Notion plan to use this?

No. This template works fully on Notion's free plan. All formulas, databases, and views are compatible with free accounts.

---

❓ Can I use this for multiple platforms like Etsy and Shopify at the same time?

Yes. The Platform Fee % column lets you create duplicate entries for the same product at different fee rates — so you can directly compare your Etsy vs. Shopify margins side by side.

---

❓ What currency does this use?

The template is set up in USD by default. Because Notion uses plain number fields, you can apply it to any currency (GBP, EUR, AUD, etc.) — simply treat the numbers as your local currency.

---

❓ Can I add my own product categories or custom columns?

Absolutely. Notion databases are fully customizable. Add tags, categories, seasons, or any other property that fits your business without breaking any existing formulas.

---

❓ Will this work if I sell both digital and physical products?

Yes. For digital products, simply set Shipping Cost and Packaging Cost to 0. The remaining formula will calculate your profit margin on digital listings accurately.
